# Four-Part Slide Structure

Use this structure for a paper-reading 组会汇报. A 15-20 minute talk normally needs 16-20 slides. Add or remove content pages according to paper complexity.

## 1. Cover

- Paper title in Chinese and English.
- Presenter name, advisor name, date.
- Use the generic cover and avoid institutional or personal branding.

## 2. 汇报结构

Show four cards:

1. 研究背景
2. 主要内容
3. 方法与评价标准
4. 实验设计与我的理解

## 3. Part 1 — 研究背景

Use a section divider, then one or two content slides.

Include:

- The broader task and why it matters.
- What previous methods or scaling assumptions do.
- The empirical anomaly or limitation that motivates the paper.
- The exact research question.
- A one-sentence hypothesis.

For speech/audio papers, connect to world models, AVSE, AVSS, spatial audio, or embodied hearing when relevant.

## 4. Part 2 — 主要内容

Use a section divider, then one or two content slides.

Include:

- What the paper proposes.
- The input, output, and target task.
- The core conclusion.
- The most important evidence.
- What is genuinely new versus inherited from prior work.

Recommended split:

- Page A: paper overview and core conclusion.
- Page B: data/problem analysis or the central phenomenon.

## 5. Part 3 — 方法与评价标准

Use a section divider, then two or three content slides.

Recommended split:

- Page A: overall method pipeline.
- Page B: key modules, filtering rules, loss, or equations.
- Page C: evaluation metrics, test sets, and why the evaluation design is credible.

Do not mix a dense method diagram, threshold table, and metric taxonomy on one slide.

For metrics, separate:

- Non-intrusive metrics: no clean reference needed.
- Intrusive metrics: clean reference needed.
- Metrics used during filtering versus held-out metrics.
- Quality metrics versus fidelity/intelligibility metrics.

## 6. Part 4 — 实验设计与我的理解

Use a section divider, then three or four content slides plus Q&A.

Recommended split:

- Page A: experimental setup, data, models, and comparison arms.
- Page B: main results and direct comparison.
- Page C: additional experiments, ablations, scaling, or failure cases.
- Page D: connection to the configured research directions and personal takeaway.
- Final page: Q&A.

The research-connection page must state direct relevance, transferable method, one experiment idea, and one limitation. The personal takeaway should answer:

- What did I learn?
- Which conclusion changes my future experiments?
- How does it connect to AVSE/AVSS or world models?
- What evidence is still missing?

## Speaker-note style

Each page should have a speaker note that:

- Starts with the page's main point.
- Explains the evidence in direct spoken Chinese.
- Uses one or two concrete numbers when available.
- Distinguishes “论文验证” from “我的推断”.
- Ends only if a transition is useful; do not force every page to end with a slogan.

## Page-density rules

- One main claim per slide.
- Avoid more than three dense text groups.
- Tables should show the minimum comparison needed.
- Use native charts only when the values are complete and traceable.
- Put detailed explanations in speaker notes rather than on the slide.
